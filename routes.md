# Loop Watch routes (Phase 1)

24 origin/destination pairs (14 tunnel / future-tunnel comparisons, including a two-direction Westgate–Airport pair, a Paradise Road waypoint variant in each direction, and a 4744 Paradise Road–Airport surface-leg pair in each direction, plus 6 rail-section pairs). Coordinates were looked up from OpenStreetMap (Nominatim and Photon) on Monday, Nov 23, 2026 and Wednesday, Nov 25, 2026 (America/New_York), with the Westgate–Airport pair, Paradise Road waypoint, and 4744 Paradise Road surface-leg pair added Friday, Nov 27, 2026. Airport points use the terminal or curb, not the airfield reference point. Two Tropicana Avenue intersections were constructed from independently sourced Tropicana latitudes and Decatur / Rainbow longitudes; see the source notes in `routes.json`. Most pairs are sampled origin → destination only, to stay inside TomTom's free monthly routing cap. The Westgate–Airport future-tunnel pair is sampled in both directions, and a second pair in each direction is forced via a Paradise Road waypoint at Thomas & Mack latitude so the street the new tunnels parallel is timed separately from TomTom's fastest route.

Public-facing names are in the **Name** column. Do not describe Orlando routes as "near I-Drive" in posts or other public copy.

Machine-readable coordinates and source notes are in `routes.json`.

**Types and roles.** *Tunnel* (`role: treated`) means an operating Loop could already be substituting for the surface trip (Vegas only). *Future Tunnel* (`role: baseline`) means collect now so a future before/after is possible: Nashville, Orlando, and the Westgate–Airport / Paradise Road segment in Las Vegas, where the new twin tunnels are not yet open to the public. *Rail* (`role: treated` in the rail comparison) means a road trip alongside a rail alternative. *Road-Only* (`role: control`) means a comparable corridor the Loop or rail line does not serve, used for difference-in-differences.

**Timezones and rush-hour windows.** Las Vegas: `America/Los_Angeles`, weekdays 6:30–9:30 a.m. and 3:30–6:30 p.m. PT. Nashville: `America/Chicago`, weekdays 7:00–9:30 a.m. and 3:30–6:30 p.m. CT. Orlando: `America/New_York`, weekdays 7:00–9:30 a.m. and 4:00–6:30 p.m. ET. A 15-minute grace after each window end covers a late cron and is not an extra planned sample. Weekends are skipped.
